Privacy Policy

Belagavi Smart Tourism
Effective Date: January 1, 2025 · Version 1.0
Our Commitment Belagavi Smart Tourism is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide smart tourism services, personalized destination recommendations, and budget tracking. We never sell your personal information to third parties.
Location (GPS) Usage We request your location solely to provide real-time distance and travel time to tourist landmarks, dynamic route navigation, and local weather forecasts. Your live GPS coordinates are processed on your device and are never stored or shared with external parties. You can deny location access at any time.
Authentication & Storage Authentication is secured by Google Firebase Authentication. We use Google Cloud Firestore to store your personal wishlists, trip expense records, and conversation history. All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS encryption.
Your Rights & Control You can edit your personal details, clear your saved places and expenses, or permanently delete your account and all associated personal records at any time from the Settings screen.
